How We Work

Starting a project with Yantratmika Solutions is simple and straightforward. We follow a proven process to ensure successful outcomes:

1. Discovery Call

We start with a conversation to understand your business, challenges, and goals. This helps us determine the best approach for your project.

2. Proposal

Based on our discussion, we create a detailed proposal outlining the solution architecture, timeline, and investment required.

3. Planning

Once approved, we work together to create a detailed project plan with milestones, deliverables, and success criteria.

4. Development

Our team builds your solution using agile methodology with regular updates and demos to ensure alignment.

5. Testing & QA

Rigorous testing ensures quality, security, and performance before deployment.

6. Deployment & Support

We deploy your solution and provide ongoing support to ensure smooth operation and continuous improvement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What industries do you work with?

We work with clients across various industries including finance, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, logistics, and technology startups. Our solutions are customized to meet specific industry requirements.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A small MVP might take 6-8 weeks, while enterprise solutions can take 3-6 months or longer. We'll provide a detailed timeline in our proposal.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes, we offer various support and maintenance packages to ensure your solution continues to perform optimally. We can also provide on-demand support as needed.

Can you work with our existing team?

Absolutely! We're experienced in collaborating with in-house teams, whether augmenting your development capacity or working alongside your technical staff.

What is your pricing model?

We offer flexible pricing models including fixed-price projects, time and materials, and dedicated team arrangements. The best model depends on your project requirements and we'll discuss options in our proposal.
